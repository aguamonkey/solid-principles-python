# Decision · capture evidence before another supplier call

[Lab](README.md)

Require a stable catalogue only where the client relies on one. Copy returned identities/titles into value objects before sampling again, then check both the earlier projection and new result. This detects observable aliasing without claiming general purity.

Initial unavailability is a documented outcome. After success, instability or loss of availability violates this chosen promise. A weaker live-source abstraction may be appropriate for another consumer. Structural typing alone cannot make that choice.
